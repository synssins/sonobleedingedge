## Technical Notes

### Cross-Brand Speaker Synchronization
Research completed: Cross-brand speaker synchronization (e.g., Sonos + AirPlay + Chromecast simultaneously in sync) is not feasible due to:
- No standardized timestamp protocol across different speaker ecosystems
- Different latency compensation methods per protocol
- Same-protocol sync works well (Sonos-to-Sonos, AirPlay-to-AirPlay)
- Cross-protocol sync requires proprietary bridges (not implementable in Sonorium)
